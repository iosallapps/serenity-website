Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Serenity: AI Mood Journal ("Serenity", "we", "our") is a journaling app for iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, who receives it and the choices you have. It describes the app as it ships, version 1.13 and later.

1. What stays on your iPhone

Your journal lives in the app's own database on your device. There is no Serenity account, and your entries are never uploaded, backed up or synced by us. This includes:

  • Journal entries: what you write, the mood you pick, tags, photos you attach and the time of each entry
  • Voice entries: recordings and their transcripts, made with Apple's on-device speech recognition
  • Notes, gratitude entries, streaks and insights: computed on the device from your own entries
  • Settings: reminders, Face ID or Touch ID lock and other preferences

The data is protected by iOS Data Protection with your device passcode. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. What leaves your iPhone, and why

2.1 AI prompts and reflections

When you ask for an AI prompt, Serenity sends your chosen mood and the app's language. When you ask for a Mirror reflection, it also sends the first 200 characters of that entry and 50 character previews of your last 7 entries. No entry is ever sent in full. These requests go over HTTPS to our own server (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards them to Groq to generate the text. Our server receives a random install number, used only to count requests against a monthly limit; it carries no name, account or device identifier. Neither we nor our server store the content of the request. Groq processes it under its own terms: groq.com/privacy-policy. If you do not use the AI features, nothing from your journal leaves the device.

2.2 Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: an ad when you open the app and, at most once a minute, after you save an entry or a note. Ads are never shown with crisis resources. Serenity Pro removes all ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad or measurement starts. You can change your choice later in Settings, under Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not, ads are not personalised with it.
  • Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described in Google's policy. Nothing you write in Serenity is ever shared with Google or any advertiser.

2.3 Analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. They run only where your consent choice allows it (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, through the consent form above), and you can turn them off at any time in Settings. They receive usage events (for example "an entry was saved" or "the paywall was shown"), device and app information and crash logs, never the text of your entries, notes, recordings or searches.

2.4 Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to know whether you have Serenity Pro: it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts from Apple, not your name or payment details.

2.5 App Store campaign attribution

Once, after the first launch, the app reads Apple's attribution token (AdServices) to learn whether the install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign. It contains no personal data.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we never use your journal for advertising.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and there is no sign up.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not store your entries on any server.

4. Your choices

  • Ads and tracking: answer the consent form and the tracking prompt as you wish; change them in Settings (Ad Privacy Settings) and in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Analytics: turn Anonymous Analytics off in Settings.
  • AI features: optional; the journal works fully without them.
  • Your data: export your journal from History or Settings, delete single entries, or delete everything in Settings.
  • App lock: turn Face ID or Touch ID protection on or off in Settings.

5. Crisis resources

Serenity can recognise words that may signal a crisis and show support resources. This check runs entirely on your device and nothing about it is sent anywhere.

6. Children

Serenity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. International transfers

AI requests, analytics, crash reports and ad requests may be processed in countries other than yours by Cloudflare, Groq, Google and RevenueCat, under their own safeguards. Your journal itself stays on your device.

8. Retention

We keep no copy of your journal. AI request content is not stored by us. Analytics, crash and advertising data are kept by Google under its retention settings; purchase records by Apple and RevenueCat as required for your purchases.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and the date above when the app changes what it collects, and mention material changes in the app.

11. In short

  • Your journal stays on your iPhone. No account, no upload, no sync.
  • AI features send only your mood, your language and short excerpts you ask about, never a full entry.
  • The free version has ads from Google AdMob, personalised only with your consent; Serenity Pro has none.
  • Analytics and crash reports follow your consent and can be turned off in Settings.
  • We never sell your data.
