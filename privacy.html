1. Who we are

Visto Apps is the brand of Darius Cirjan, the developer and data controller. This policy explains what the Serenity: AI Mood Journal app (iOS) and the website https://serenityjournalapp.com do with personal data.

Contact for any privacy question or request: iosallapps@gmail.com. Website: vistoapps.com. We answer within one month (art. 12 GDPR). We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer.

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

2. At a glance

  • No account. Serenity does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your journal stays on your iPhone: entries, moods, photos, voice recordings and notes. We have no copy of it, and nothing is synced to iCloud.
  • The free version sends nothing from your journal to any AI service. In Serenity Pro, AI prompts and reflections send your mood, a goal you gave in onboarding and short excerpts of entries (never a full entry) through a small relay we run on Cloudflare to Groq, a US company.
  • Voice entries are transcribed by Apple's speech recognition, which may use Apple's servers.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Personalised ads only with your consent; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland no ad loads before you answer the consent form. Serenity Pro removes ads. Nothing you write is used for ads.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) are pseudonymous, follow your consent choice where the law requires it and can be turned off in Settings, Anonymous Analytics. They never contain your moods or text.
  • We do not sell personal data for money. The only server of ours that touches your data is the AI relay, which does not store it.

3. Data that stays on your device

Entries, the mood you pick, notes, gratitude entries, tags, photos, voice recordings and their transcripts, streaks, insights and settings are stored only in the app's own database and files on your iPhone, with iOS Data Protection. There is no Serenity account and no cloud sync: we have no copy and cannot read them. Insights, patterns and the crisis check are computed on the device. Moods and entries can reveal your state of mind and sometimes health information, which the law treats as especially sensitive; that is why they stay on the device, and the only things that can leave it are listed in the table below. In the app, Settings, Export My Data gives you a copy, Clear All History removes your entries, and Settings, Delete All My Data erases everything; deleting the app removes it all too.

5. Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use the feature, and works without them. Change them anytime in your device settings.

  • Microphone. Only while you record a voice entry. The recording is saved on your device.
  • Speech recognition. To transcribe a voice entry. See Apple speech recognition above for what Apple may process.
  • Photos. Only the photos you pick to attach to an entry. They are stored on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Face ID or Touch ID. Optional app lock. The check is done by iOS; the app never receives your face or fingerprint data.
  • Notifications. Local reminders scheduled on the device. No push server is involved.
  • Tracking (iOS, App Tracking Transparency). Asked once, after the consent form. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.

6. Consent, ads and tracking

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Until you answer it, no ad is requested and no usage statistics are sent. If you refuse personalised ads, you still get limited, non-personalised ads.

On iOS the App Tracking Transparency prompt comes after the consent form. Ads load only once it is answered.

Change your choice anytime: in the app, Settings, Ad Privacy Settings (shown where the consent form applies). Anonymous Analytics, in Settings, switches analytics and crash reports off at any time, in every country. On iOS, also Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Buying the paid version removes ads.

7. Purchases

Purchases are made through App Store. The store processes the payment; we receive only a pseudonymous receipt, checked through RevenueCat, that tells the app which purchase you own. We never see your card or account details.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app (Settings, Clear All History or Delete All My Data) or delete the app. We keep no copy of your journal. The AI relay stores no request text; it keeps only a per-install request counter that expires after about 40 days. Data held by the other services above is kept for the period listed in the table. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to answer, and at most 2 years.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (Google, RevenueCat and others marked in the table) process data in the United States or other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (art. 45 and 46 GDPR).

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing (including to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Your journal is only on your device, so you already hold it: Settings, Export My Data gives you a copy and Delete All My Data erases it. Because the app has no account, we usually cannot tell which data at Google or RevenueCat is yours. Write to iosallapps@gmail.com and include, if you can, the date of your install or purchase and the country, and we will forward the request to the provider.

You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work, or to ours: Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro. The list of EU authorities: edpb.europa.eu.

11. United States

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • When you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob uses your advertising identifier for ads based on activity in other apps. Some US state laws (for example California) call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time: refuse tracking on iOS (Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking), or email us and we will treat it as an opt-out request.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and will not be treated differently for doing so. Categories collected in the last 12 months are the ones in the table above (identifiers, internet activity, approximate location from IP, purchase records). Requests: iosallapps@gmail.com.

12. Children

Serenity: AI Mood Journal is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16 in the EU). If you believe a child gave us data, write to iosallapps@gmail.com and we will delete what we can reach. Ads in the app are not designed for children.

13. Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Data on the device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what leaves your device to the minimum listed here.

14. This website

serenityjournalapp.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which records the IP address of visitors in server logs for security. The site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and uses no analytics or ads.

15. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships. Important changes are also mentioned in the store update notes.
