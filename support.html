Support & FAQ

Answers about Serenity: AI Mood Journal. Cannot find yours? Email us.

Getting started

How do I write my first entry?

Open Serenity and tap Write in my journal on the Home tab (or Check in for a quick mood log). Pick how you feel, read the prompt, then write or record your thoughts. Add tags or a photo if you like and tap Save.

Which moods can I choose?

Ten: Happy, Calm, Grateful, Excited, Neutral, Anxious, Sad, Angry, Tired and Stressed. Insights, the mood calendar and the trend chart are built from the moods you log.

Do I need an account?

No. Serenity has no sign up and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your journal lives on your iPhone.

Free and Serenity Pro

What is free and what is Pro?

Serenity is free to download and shows ads. The free version has limits: three voice recordings of up to two minutes, three quick notes and three prompts a day from a built-in library. Quick mood check-ins have no limit. Serenity Pro removes ads and these limits and writes your prompts and reflections with AI. It is a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase; the price and any free trial are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Serenity go to Settings, Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Serenity. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Pro but it is locked, or I have a new phone.

Use Restore Purchases on the Serenity Pro screen, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still does not unlock, write to us with the date of purchase.

Privacy and your data

Where is my journal stored?

Only on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection. There is no Serenity account, we have no copy of your entries, and nothing is synced to iCloud. Your iPhone's own backup can include the app's data, as with most apps; that is controlled by iOS settings.

What does the AI see?

Only in Serenity Pro. For a prompt, the app sends the mood you picked, the app language and, if you gave them in onboarding, your goal and how predictable your mood is. For a Mirror reflection after you save an entry, it sends that entry's mood and first 200 characters plus the mood and first 50 characters of your last 7 entries. Never a full entry. The request goes through a small relay we run on Cloudflare to Groq, a US company, and the relay does not store it. The free version sends nothing to AI. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

What happens to my voice recordings?

The recording is saved on your iPhone. To turn speech into text, Apple's speech recognition processes the audio, and depending on your device and language Apple may do that on its servers, under Apple's privacy policy.

Which analytics and ads does Serenity use?

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Usage statistics and crash reports use Google Firebase and never include your moods or text. Where the law requires it, you answer Google's consent form first, and you can change it in Settings, Ad Privacy Settings. In every country you can switch statistics and crash reports off with Settings, Anonymous Analytics. If iOS asks to track you across apps, refusing means your device identifier is not shared for ads.

How do I lock the app with Face ID?

Go to Settings and turn on Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID, depending on your iPhone). Serenity then asks for it when you return to the app.

How do I export or delete my data?

To export, go to Settings, Export My Data, or in History open the filter menu and choose Export CSV or Export Text. To remove your entries, use Settings, Clear All History. To erase everything and start over, use Settings, Delete All My Data. These cannot be undone, and deleting the app also removes the data.

Using Serenity

Can I use Serenity on more than one device?

Each iPhone keeps its own journal, because nothing is synced. To move your entries, export them from Settings, Export My Data.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts the days in a row you save an entry or a quick check-in. A missed day starts a new streak, but your entries, badges and longest streak stay.

When do Insights unlock?

After you log at least 7 entries. Insights then show your best and hardest day, the time of day you feel best, your mood trend and how steady it is. All of it is calculated on your device.

What is Crisis Detection?

An optional feature that is off until you turn it on in Settings. It then checks entries written in English or Romanian, on your device, and shows support resources for your region if it finds signs of crisis. It is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services.

How do reminders work?

Turn on Daily Reminders in Settings and allow notifications when iOS asks. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; there is no push server.

Troubleshooting

AI prompts are not appearing.

AI prompts and reflections are part of Serenity Pro and need an internet connection. If the request fails, Serenity shows a prompt from its built-in library instead, so you can keep writing. Check your connection, reopen the app and update to the latest version.

The app crashes or will not open.
  1. Force-quit the app and open it again.
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Update iOS and Serenity.
  4. Free up storage in Settings, General, iPhone Storage.

If it continues, email us with what you were doing when it happened.

Still need help?

Write to iosallapps@gmail.com and tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.
