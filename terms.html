2. What Serenity: AI Mood Journal does

Serenity is a mood journal and diary for iPhone. You pick a mood, get a journaling prompt, write or speak your entry, and see insights, streaks and a mood calendar built from your entries. It can lock the app with Face ID, remind you to write, and, if you turn it on, check entries for signs of crisis and show support resources.
